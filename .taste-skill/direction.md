# The Living Tarot redesign

Software. The app gives moms and homemakers a personal tarot reading on their own time. The first action is **Start a reading**.

The deal was **Clay and pine**, **Outfit**, the **product-stage hero**, and **word-highlight**. The warm ground gives the historical card art room and pine identifies actions and selected controls. The stage shows the app's real spread component with its Rider–Waite–Smith artwork. Outfit is self-hosted with its license. The mono face is unused because the app has no code or data requiring it.

- Landing: headline and actions over a Reading Table preview. The next screen explains what adding context does after a reading.
- Workspace: common frame with a desktop navigation rail and phone navigation in two rows. Profile is accessible from the header.
- Reading Table: spread and question side by side at wide widths, actions and preview below.
- Reader: interpretation and guidance lead. Cards and conversation sit in a companion column at wide widths.
- Lexicon, Archive, Life Memory, Patterns, and Symbol Journal: shared fields, controls and surfaces with layouts that fit their content.
- Profile, setup, and card and Gemini dialogs: consistent focus, borders, touch targets, and responsive containment.

Existing routes, state, submission payloads, and data storage are retained. Inspection uses temporary browser profiles, fictional data, and a local Gemini fixture.
